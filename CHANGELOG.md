# Changelog

## 1.0.0 (22 Nov 2026)

First public release.

- Crystal wyrms drop glowing Crystal Leather in their own color (blue, green or purple). The more of it an item has, the brighter it glows.
- Green crystal wyrms from level 50 and purple ones from level 100, getting more common the deeper you go. Both are bigger and tougher, with their own behavior and colored spit.
- Every wyrm in the mines gets more health, damage and attack speed the deeper it is, up to level 150.
- Server console commands: `wyrmvar list`, `odds`, `spawn`, `clear` and `verbose`.
